01 / 05

AuditIQ — Financial AI Agent

Autonomous AI-powered financial auditing & forecasting platform

AuditIQ modernises corporate auditing. Upload a ledger and a multi-agent pipeline parses it, flags anomalies, forecasts the next period and writes the compliance narrative — in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. I designed, built and deployed it end to end: the model pipeline, the FastAPI back end and the web interface.

View Live App API Docs GitHub

The problem

Traditional corporate auditing is slow, manual and error-prone.

01
Manual bottlenecks

Auditors comb through thousands of ledger entries by hand, spending days of skilled labour on routine review.

02
Human oversight errors

Fatigue and volume lead to missed anomalies, discrepancies and compliance risks that surface too late.

03
High processing latency

Legacy workflows can't keep pace with the real-time financial decisions modern businesses need to make.

Core capabilities

Four pillars behind end-to-end intelligent auditing.

A
Ledger review & parsing

Ingests Excel, CSV and PDF ledgers, plus receipt images read through a vision-capable LLM.

B
Anomaly detection

Surfaces irregularities and discrepancies the moment data is ingested and verifies true anomalies against normal variance.

C
Predictive forecasting

Regression models and weighted multi-period revenue metrics project fiscal-quarter outcomes.

D
Multilingual reporting

Audit-ready narratives and PDF reports with charts, in English, Roman Urdu and Urdu.

Supply Chain & Warehouse Management System

DBMS project · SQL · PostgreSQL

  • Order management with status tracking (pending, processing, shipped, delivered)
  • Inventory ledger that records receipts and deducts stock automatically per sale
